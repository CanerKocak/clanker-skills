# Audit PDF structure (reference)

## OWASP WSTG-style executive summary

- Objective of the test  
- Business need  
- Key findings in business language (no deep tech in exec summary)  
- Strategic recommendations  

## Spearbit-style severity

Impact × likelihood matrix → Critical / High / Medium / Low (+ optional Gas for contracts).

## Trail of Bits-style extras (optional pages)

- Project targets / coverage table  
- Summary of recommendations  
- Tooling / automated results appendix  

## Ruled-out section

Firms that look serious list **what they tried and failed to break**. Keep this.
It builds trust more than extra Mediums.
